# Operations Handbook

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

# Purpose

This handbook defines the operational model of HSDD.

It translates identity and governance into daily execution.

The objective is to maintain consistency, quality, and continuity across all activities.

---

# Operational Philosophy

HSDD operates through systems rather than isolated tasks.

Every activity should contribute to at least one of the following:

- Knowledge
- Assets
- Narrative
- Culture
- Revenue

Work that contributes to none of these should be reconsidered.

---

# Core Operating Domains

HSDD currently operates through five domains.

## 1. Identity

Maintains:

- Enterprise Identity
- Positioning
- Strategic Direction

Outputs:

- Identity Documents
- Strategic Narratives

---

## 2. Governance

Maintains:

- Decision Frameworks
- Policies
- Standards

Outputs:

- Governance Documents
- Internal Standards

---

## 3. Operations

Maintains:

- Day-to-day activities
- Execution workflows
- Project coordination

Outputs:

- Operational Procedures
- Workflows

---

## 4. Assets

Maintains:

- Knowledge Assets
- Creative Assets
- Digital Assets

Outputs:

- Reusable Intellectual Property
- Documentation
- Archives

---

## 5. Systems

Maintains:

- Style Systems
- Spatial Culture Systems
- Narrative Systems
- AI Collaboration Systems

Outputs:

- Frameworks
- Modules
- Specifications

---

# Daily Operations Framework

Daily work should follow:

```text
Collect
    ↓
Organize
    ↓
Create
    ↓
Document
    ↓
Archive
```

Every meaningful activity should generate documentation.

---

# Project Lifecycle

All projects move through the following stages.

## Draft

Research and exploration.

Location:

```text
DRAFTS/
```

---

## Development

Active work and refinement.

Location:

```text
PROJECTS/
or
SYSTEMS/
```

---

## Active

Approved for operational use.

Location:

```text
OPERATIONS/
ASSETS/
```

---

## Archived

Retained for future reference.

Location:

```text
ARCHIVE/
```

---

# Documentation Standard

Documents should be:

- Clear
- Concise
- Versioned
- Searchable

Naming Convention:

```text
Document-Name-v1.0.md
Document-Name-v1.1.md
Document-Name-v2.0.md
```

Avoid:

- Untitled files
- Duplicate documents
- Multiple competing versions

---

# Knowledge Management

Knowledge should be captured before it is forgotten.

Priority:

```text
Conversation
    ↓
Notes
    ↓
Document
    ↓
System
```

The goal is to convert temporary thinking into reusable knowledge.

---

# Asset Management

An item becomes an HSDD Asset when:

- It can be reused
- It creates value
- It has documentation
- It can survive independently of its creator

Asset examples:

- Frameworks
- Operating Procedures
- Design Systems
- Knowledge Bases
- AI Workflows

---

# AI Collaboration

AI is treated as a system collaborator.

AI may assist with:

- Documentation
- Analysis
- Structuring
- Brainstorming
- Knowledge organization

Human judgment remains responsible for final decisions.

---

# Quality Principles

Before an output is finalized, ask:

1. Is it understandable?
2. Is it reusable?
3. Is it documented?
4. Is it aligned with identity?
5. Is it worth maintaining?

If not, improve it before publishing.

---

# Operational Success

Success is measured by:

- Clarity
- Continuity
- Reusability
- Documentation quality
- Long-term value creation

Not by activity volume alone.

---

# Relationship to Governance

```text
Identity
    ↓
Governance
    ↓
Operations
    ↓
Assets
```

Operations execute governance.

Governance protects identity.

Identity defines direction.
