# Corporate Culture

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

# Purpose

This document defines the cultural foundation of HSDD.

Culture determines how decisions are interpreted, how work is approached, and how long-term continuity is maintained.

Governance provides structure.

Culture provides direction.

---

# Core Belief

HSDD believes that organizations are built through people, systems, and stories.

Culture exists to align all three.

---

# Cultural Principles

## 1. Protect Your Awake Time

Time is the most limited resource.

Work should focus on what is meaningful, valuable, and worth pursuing.

Avoid spending energy on activities that do not contribute to growth, learning, value creation, or continuity.

---

## 2. Build Rather Than Follow

HSDD values original creation over imitation.

The goal is not to compete on replication.

The goal is to build systems, ideas, and experiences that reflect our own perspective.

---

## 3. Long-Term Thinking

Short-term results matter.

Long-term continuity matters more.

Decisions should consider future maintainability and future value.

---

## 4. Clarity Over Noise

Clear communication is preferred over complexity.

Simple ideas that can be maintained are more valuable than complicated ideas that cannot.

---

## 5. Documentation Creates Continuity

Memory fades.

Documentation remains.

Important knowledge should be documented, structured, and preserved.

---

# The 80/20 Principle

Not all activities create equal value.

HSDD encourages prioritizing the few actions that create the greatest long-term impact.

Questions:

- What is the critical 20%?
- What creates lasting value?
- What should be ignored?

---

# The "Be Yourself" Principle

Many systems encourage conformity.

HSDD encourages authenticity.

Projects should reflect:

- Original thinking
- Personal responsibility
- Independent judgment

The objective is not to resemble others.

The objective is to become more aligned with oneself.

---

# The Game Design Mindset

Work should be approached as a meaningful game.

A healthy project combines:

- Challenge
- Curiosity
- Creativity
- Collaboration

Projects that contain none of these should be reconsidered.

---

# Habit Framework

HSDD uses a simple habit model.

```text
Trigger
    ↓
Action
    ↓
Reward
```

Sustainable systems emerge from repeated behaviors.

Culture is the accumulation of habits over time.

---

# Learning Culture

Every project should generate learning.

Every failure should generate knowledge.

Every success should generate reusable systems.

Learning is considered an operational asset.

---

# Collaboration Culture

HSDD values:

- Respect
- Openness
- Curiosity
- Constructive disagreement

The objective is not consensus.

The objective is better understanding.

---

# Creative Culture

Creation is preferred over consumption.

Whenever possible:

```text
Observe
    ↓
Think
    ↓
Create
    ↓
Document
```

Creative output becomes organizational knowledge.

---

# Relationship to Governance

```text
Identity
    ↓
Governance
    ↓
Culture
    ↓
Operations
```

Identity defines who we are.

Governance defines how we decide.

Culture defines how we behave.

Operations define how we work.

---

# Cultural Objective

The objective of culture is not motivation.

The objective of culture is continuity.

A strong culture helps preserve:

- Identity
- Quality
- Direction
- Learning
- Long-term value

through changing circumstances.
