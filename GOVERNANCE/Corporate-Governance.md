# Corporate Governance

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

# Purpose

This document defines how HSDD makes decisions, maintains alignment, and preserves long-term continuity.

Governance exists to ensure that growth does not compromise identity, quality, or direction.

---

# Governance Principles

All governance activities follow five principles:

1. Clarity Over Complexity
2. Long-Term Over Short-Term
3. Systems Over Dependency
4. Consistency Over Reaction
5. Continuity Over Fragmentation

---

# Governance Structure

HSDD operates through a dual-core governance model.

## Human Core

MIN

Responsibilities:

- Vision
- Direction
- Strategy
- Narrative
- Identity

MIN determines:

- Why something should exist
- Whether something aligns with HSDD
- What should be prioritized

---

## System Core

AI Systems

Responsibilities:

- Organization
- Documentation
- Structuring
- Knowledge Management
- Continuity Support

AI assists with:

- Decision support
- Information synthesis
- Process consistency
- Repository maintenance

---

# Decision Framework

Decisions should be evaluated through the following hierarchy.

## Level 1

Identity

Question:

Does this align with HSDD identity?

---

## Level 2

System

Question:

Can this be supported by a repeatable system?

---

## Level 3

Operations

Question:

Can this be executed sustainably?

---

## Level 4

Assets

Question:

Will this create long-term value?

---

# Repository Governance

All files belong to one of four categories.

## Foundation

Core identity and governance documents.

Examples:

- Enterprise Identity
- Corporate Governance
- Corporate Culture

---

## Operations

Documents that support execution.

Examples:

- Operations Handbook
- Workflows
- Procedures

---

## Assets

Documents representing reusable value.

Examples:

- Style Systems
- Spatial Culture
- Knowledge Assets

---

## Drafts

Research and experimental ideas.

Draft documents are not considered official standards.

---

# Change Management

Changes should follow a version-first approach.

Examples:

```text
v1.0
v1.1
v1.2
v2.0
```

Major documents should not be overwritten.

New versions should be created when:

- Structure changes
- Scope changes
- Governance changes

Minor corrections may update an existing version.

---

# Authority Model

The following order applies.

Identity
    ↓
Governance
    ↓
Operations
    ↓
Projects
    ↓
Outputs

Lower levels may not contradict higher levels.

---

# Governance Boundaries

HSDD will not pursue:

- Activities that weaken identity
- Unstructured growth
- Documentation without purpose
- Systems without maintenance
- Projects without ownership

---

# Governance Objective

The objective of governance is not control.

The objective of governance is continuity.

Success is measured by the ability to maintain:

- Identity
- Clarity
- Quality
- Adaptability
- Long-term operation

across time.
