# Narrative Matrix

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Owner: HSDD

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Narrative Matrix framework used within HSDD.

The Narrative Matrix provides a systematic approach for transforming ideas, experiences, spaces, assets, and systems into coherent narratives.

Narrative is treated as an organizational asset rather than a marketing activity.

---

# Definition

A Narrative Matrix is a framework that connects:

- Identity
- Style
- Space
- Behavior
- Experience
- Assets

into meaningful and reusable stories.

---

# Core Principle

Stories emerge from systems.

Narratives are not invented.

Narratives are discovered through observation and interpretation.

---

# Narrative Formula

```text
Identity
    ↓
Style
    ↓
Space
    ↓
Experience
    ↓
Story
    ↓
Narrative
```

Strong narratives emerge when all layers remain aligned.

---

# Narrative Layers

## Layer 1 — Identity

Answers:

- Who are we?
- Why do we exist?

Reference:

- Enterprise Identity

---

## Layer 2 — Style

Answers:

- How do we think?
- How do we communicate?

Reference:

- Style Language
- Style Engineering

---

## Layer 3 — Space

Answers:

- Where does the experience occur?
- What environment shapes the experience?

Reference:

- Spatial Culture

---

## Layer 4 — Experience

Answers:

- What happens?
- What is observed?

Experiences create narrative material.

---

## Layer 5 — Story

Answers:

- What is memorable?
- What should be shared?

Stories organize experiences into understandable forms.

---

## Layer 6 — Narrative

Answers:

- What meaning is created?
- What values are reinforced?

Narratives shape culture over time.

# Narrative Quality Criteria

A narrative should be:

- Authentic
- Observable
- Reusable
- Transferable
- Aligned with identity

Avoid narratives that rely on:

- Exaggeration
- Hype
- Ambiguity
- Artificial complexity
